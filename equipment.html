Earthmoving Machinery Categories

The equipment types below represent the primary machinery categories required for comprehensive excavation projects. Exact makes, models, operating weights, and attachment configurations are confirmed according to individual site geological assessments and project scope.

Core Excavation Plant

Excavators

Crawler and wheeled hydraulic excavators suitable for mass soil extraction, deep foundation pits, rock fracture hammering, and slope cutting.

Category Class:Heavy Crawler & Wheeled Units
Representative Weight:20 – 50 Metric Tons
Bucket Capacities:1.2 – 3.2 m³ Rock / Soil Buckets
Auxiliary Tooling:Hydraulic Rock Breakers & Rippers
Typical Applications:Basement pits, mass cut, rock breaking

Earthmoving & Ripping Plant

Bulldozers

Heavy crawler tractors designed for land clearing, thick brush grubbing, mass soil pushing, hard ground ripping, and initial site leveling.

Category Class:Heavy Crawler Tractors
Representative Power:200 – 350+ HP Class
Ripper Attachment:Single / Multi-shank Rippers
Blade Configuration:Semi-U & Angle Dozer Blades
Typical Applications:Mass earth push, site clearing, ripping

Material Handling Plant

Wheel Loaders

High-capacity front-end wheel loaders for rapid loading of excavated earth and rock into dump trucks and bulk stockpiling.

Category Class:Heavy Articulated Wheel Loaders
Bucket Range:3.0 – 5.0 m³ Material Buckets
Operating Weight:18 – 28 Metric Tons
Drivetrain:4-Wheel Drive Heavy Axles
Typical Applications:Truck loading, soil transfer, stockpiling

Haulage & Transport Plant

Dump Trucks

Rigid tippers and articulated off-road dump trucks for bulk spoil hauling, surplus earth removal, and cut-and-fill soil redistribution.

Category Class:Heavy Tippers & Articulated Haulers
Haul Capacity:16 – 40 Metric Tons / 15 – 24 m³
Drive Configuration:6x4, 8x4, and 6x6 Off-Road
Body Type:Reinforced Hardox Steel Rock Bodies
Typical Applications:Spoil disposal, off-site hauling, fill transport

Grading & Profiling Plant

Motor Graders

Articulated motor graders designed for precise cut-and-fill balance, road earthwork subgrade shaping, slope battering, and land leveling.

Category Class:Heavy Articulated Motor Graders
Moldboard Length:12 – 14 ft (3.6 – 4.3 m) Blades
Grade Control:Laser & Ultrasonic Sensor Adaptable
Operating Weight:15 – 19 Metric Tons
Typical Applications:Subgrade profiling, platform grading, ditching
